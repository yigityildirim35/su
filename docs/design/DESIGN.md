## Brand & Style

This design system crafts a sanctuary for solitary language acquisition—an intimate, serene study desk manifested as a digital PWA. Stripping away the gamified urgency, flashing streaks, and neon alarms typical of mainstream language apps, it adopts the tactile, reassuring presence of a beloved stationery notebook. 

The aesthetic marries warm paper minimalism with tactile, hand-crafted comfort. It targets a self-directed language learner building steady proficiency from A1 to B2 without cognitive fatigue. The visual landscape induces calm clarity, sustained focus, and cozy progression through organic, imperfect linework, gentle pastel tints, and clear bilingual hierarchies. Soft hand-drawn mascot motifs (an understated stick-figure line drawing paired with an expressive, round cartoon head wearing an optional party hat or reading spectacles) inject warmth without clutter. Celebratory states substitute jarring fanfare for subtle pastel ticker-tape confetti, cake vignettes, and quiet delights.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, restful study atmosphere anchored on natural desk materials and gentle chromatic accents.

- **Primary (`#8B7EB8` - Soft Lavender):** Serves as the primary focal tone for key actions, progress milestones, and English sentence grammar highlights. Conveys gentle intellect, focus, and reassurance.
- **Secondary (`#7A9E7E` - Gentle Sage Green):** Used for correct answer confirmations, active study states, vocabulary mastery indicators, and calming status bars.
- **Tertiary (`#F48C6A` - Warm Peach/Coral):** Deployed selectively for interactive focal points, review prompts, playful hints, and celebratory confetti accents.
- **Neutral (`#FFFBF5` - Warm Paper Cream):** Provides the foundational canvas tone, evoking heavy vellum or unbleached cotton notebook paper. Sub-containers and cards rest on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted paper layers (`#F7F2EA`).
- **Typography & Content Hierarchy Tokens:**
  - **Primary Ink (`#232938`):** Deep warm navy-charcoal with high contrast for English reading texts, grammatical stems, and key headwords.
  - **Turkish Gloss Ink (`#6E7382`):** Muted slate-gray for Turkish translations, lexical hints, and meta-explanations to keep secondary language presence cognitively unobtrusive.
  - **Border Ink (`#E8E0D5`):** Subtle organic card borders reflecting faint pencil guidelines.
- **Celebratory Theme Accents:** Enriched with Soft Lemon (`#FDE68A`) and Soft Sky (`#A5C4D4`) strictly for celebratory milestones and birthday micro-moments.

## Typography

The typographic system emphasizes relaxed readability and strict structural differentiation between target English inputs and native Turkish aids. 

- **Display & Headings (Quicksand):** Rounded terminals give an inviting, approachable tone to unit intros, level indicators (A1-B2), and lesson reflections.
- **Body & Labels (Nunito Sans):** Highly legible, rounded grotesque architecture suited for extended study passages and exercise options.
- **Bilingual Typographic Model:**
  - **English Text (The Target):** Always styled in `body-lg-en` or `body-md-en` using weight `600`, tinted with `#232938` for immediate optical prominence.
  - **Turkish Glosses & Hints:** Rendered systematically in `body-md-tr` or `body-sm-tr` using weight `400` and muted color `#6E7382`, nested under or beside the primary English phrase. This prevents cognitive interference while learning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces a focused single-column study pad paradigm. Rather than stretching broadly across wide screens, content is constrained to focused reading sheets to mirror a personal A5 study binder.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column fluid view with `1rem` (16px) margins and vertical stacks designed for one-handed thumb interaction.
  - **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** Centered reader layout capped at `640px` with `2rem` side padding.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** Max container width capped at `760px` centered on canvas, creating a dedicated desk surface flanked by calm negative space. Secondary navigation remains docked or off-canvas to safeguard focus.
- **Rhythm & Safe Areas:** PWA bottom safe-area insets (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`) are preserved with a minimum additional buffer of `space-md` to elevate interactive action sheets cleanly above mobile OS home indicators.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve the warm stationery feel, heavy drop shadows and high-gloss blurs are avoided in favor of tactile paper layering and gentle ambient diffusion.

- **Base Paper (Level 0):** Unbleached notebook canvas `#FFFBF5`. No shadow.
- **Notebook Cards (Level 1):** Solid white card surface `#FFFFFF` bordered with an organic 1px stroke `#E8E0D5`. Elevated via a soft, multi-stop diffuse shadow: `0 3px 12px -2px rgba(35, 41, 56, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(35, 41, 56, 0.02)`.
- **Active / Draggable Cards (Level 2):** Applied when interacting with flashcards or match-drills. Lifted via `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(139, 126, 184, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(35, 41, 56, 0.04)` accompanied by a gentle 1.5-degree rotation transform for organic tactility.
- **Modal Sheets & Overlays (Level 3):** Frosted warm backdrop overlay `rgba(255, 251, 245, 0.85)` with backdrop-filter `blur(8px)` paired with a soft bottom sheet shadow: `0 -8px 24px -6px rgba(35, 41, 56, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, protective geometry anchored at level `2` (0.5rem / 8px baseline, scaling to 1rem–1.5rem for core components). 

- **Notebook Cards & Exercise Panels:** Set strictly to `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) to convey the smooth die-cut corners of a bound pocket journal.
- **Interactive Buttons & Inputs:** Styled with `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) for approachable, finger-friendly hit regions.
- **Pills & Status Indicators:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for vocabulary tags, proficiency badges (A1, A2, B1, B2), and phonetic guides.
- **Mascot Frames:** Circular or organic asymmetric pebble shapes (`border-radius: 60% 40% 50% 50% / 50% 50% 60% 40%`) that complement the hand-drawn line character.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Continue / Submit):** Solid Soft Lavender background (`#8B7EB8`), pure white typography (`#FFFFFF`, Nunito Sans Bold 16px), `1rem` (16px) border radius, `1rem` vertical padding. Interactive press state features a soft inward physical depression: `transform: scale(0.98)` with a deeper lavender shade (`#7A6CA3`).
- **Secondary Action (Listen / Hint):** Warm cream surface with 1px border (`#E8E0D5`), Deep Navy text (`#232938`), and subtle tertiary coral accents on hover/touch.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background with Sage Green (`#7A9E7E`) text for secondary dismissal.

### Chips & Vocabulary Pills
- **Language Chips:** Fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`), padding `0.375rem 0.875rem`. 
- **Grammar/Category States:** Soft pastel fill tints at 15% opacity (`rgba(139, 126, 184, 0.15)` Lavender for nouns, `rgba(122, 158, 126, 0.18)` Sage for verbs) with coordinating solid colored text.
- **Proficiency Trackers (A1-B2):** Compact pills with border stroke and `label-sm` tracking to denote current learning stage.

### Study Cards & Flashcards
- **Structure:** Solid white `#FFFFFF` surface with `1.5rem` corner radii and subtle `1px solid #E8E0D5` border.
- **Card Content Stack:** English prompt seated prominently at the top with `headline-md` or `body-lg-en`. Direct Turkish counterpart seated cleanly below separated by `0.5rem` of whitespace, styled in `body-md-tr` (`#6E7382`).
- **Interactive State:** Minimal paper bounce on tap; flashcard flip transitions mimic turning a stiff paper index card via a 3D Y-axis rotation without synthetic metallic sheens.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Exercise Option Tiles:** Instead of bare checkmarks, options are presented as full-width interactive cards with `1rem` radius. 
- **Default State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1.5px border `#E8E0D5`.
- **Selected State:** Border `#8B7EB8`, background `rgba(139, 126, 184, 0.08)`.
- **Correct/Incorrect States:** Transitions smoothly to Sage Green (`#7A9E7E`) tint with soft checkmark icon or muted terracotta tint for gentle error review.

### Input Fields (Fill-in-the-Blank & Translation)
- **Line Notebook Inputs:** For contextual fill-in exercises, inputs adopt a playful ruled notebook style: a clean baseline border (`2px solid #E8E0D5`), zero background, generous vertical breathing space, and an auto-expanding width that snaps to focus in Soft Lavender (`#8B7EB8`).
- **Standard Translation Fields:** `rounded-lg` containers with warm white backgrounds (`#FFFBF5`), internal inset padding (`0.75rem 1rem`), and slate placeholder text.

### Mascot & Celebratory Variants
- **Line-Art Mascot Canvas:** Vector container showcasing an organic 1.5px black/navy outline stick-figure body paired with a minimal circle cartoon head. Head displays soft blushing coral cheeks (`#F48C6A`).
- **Birthday / Milestone Theme Variant:** The mascot dynamically equips an angled party hat decorated with lavender dots. Celebratory dialogs render small, slowly floating geometric confetti shards in soft pastel tints (sage, peach, lavender, soft lemon), avoiding screen shake or harsh flash effects.
